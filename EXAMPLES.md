# Before / After

All examples are made up to show the shape. Don't copy the numbers.

## Too technical (loses the recruiter)

> ❌ Enforced exactly-once order assignment via a partial unique index on active claims with
> idempotent retry semantics.

> ✅ Built a delivery dispatch tool that sends every order to **exactly one driver**, cutting
> daily planning from **2 hours to 10 minutes**.

The index and the retries are what you talk about when the interviewer asks "how?".

## Too vague (loses the engineer)

> ❌ Improved the booking system and made scheduling more efficient.

> ✅ Replaced a shared spreadsheet with an online booking page for **40 tutors**, matching
> students by course and blocking double-booked slots.

It says what module, what it does, and how the problem was solved, without the internals.
